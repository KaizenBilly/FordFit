07 Questions

Before you start.

Do I need to be fit before starting?

Not at all. Every programme is built around your current ability and confidence, whether you’re brand new to training or chasing performance.

How much does it cost?

In-person sessions start from £45 an hour, with discounted monthly packages from £160. Online coaching starts from £20 a month. No long-term contracts.

Where do sessions take place?

South Norfolk Leisure Centre in Long Stratton, NR Fitness in Wymondham, your home with Toby’s mobile gym, or online through the FordFit App.

How do I get started?

Send a message through Start Your Journey. Toby will get back to you to book a consultation and plan your first session.
